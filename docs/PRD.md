# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Problem Statement
Users face fragmented spending across banks, UPI channels, credit cards, and apps. Existing personal finance apps often require broad invasive permissions or manual expense logging.

## 2. Product Vision
CashO is a privacy-first personal finance platform that locally parses transaction SMS and emails using natural language processing (regex, tokenization, merchant normalization, TF-IDF categorization) and delivers explainable financial advice using forward-chaining expert rules and Bayesian inference.

## 3. Key Personas & Use Cases
- **Students & Young Professionals**: Track daily food delivery (Swiggy, Zomato), quick-commerce (Blinkit, Zepto), and cab rides (Uber, Ola).
- **Budget-Conscious Savers**: Receive automated alerts on dining caps, budget pacing, and unexpected recurring subscriptions.
- **Academic Evaluators**: Full transparency with reproducible lab evaluation scripts for NLP accuracy and AI rule traces.
